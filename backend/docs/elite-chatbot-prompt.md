## Product overview

Elite Ethiopia is a marketplace in Ethiopia for clients, freelancers, and sellers. In one web app, members use accounts, role-based dashboards, wallets (Birr), coins, messaging, notifications, jobs, services, digital products, contracts, reviews, and disputes.

Public pages let visitors browse services and products, explore freelancers and open jobs, and sign up. Signed-in members work in the dashboard for their role.

## Roles

- Client — post and manage jobs, review proposals, send hire invitations, run contracts, buy services and digital products, use cart and payments, earn and spend coins, message freelancers, and open disputes.
- Freelancer — publish services, browse trusted open jobs, send proposals, accept hire invitations, deliver work under contracts, track earnings in the wallet, message clients, and open disputes.
- Seller — list digital products (and product types shown in the seller portal), manage orders and inventory, view analytics, receive wallet payouts, and message buyers.
- Admin — platform operations (users, listings, disputes, settings); not a public signup role.

Accounts are role-based. Pick the role that matches what you are doing; use /contact if you need help choosing.

## Getting started

1. Sign up from the homepage and choose client, freelancer, or seller.
2. Log in and open your role dashboard.
3. Verify email when the app asks you to.
4. Complete your profile (phone where required).
5. Submit ID verification from profile if your role or action requires it; admin approval may apply.
6. For clients who want to publish open jobs: top up the wallet so payment is verified and balance rules are met.
7. Use Dashboard → Help & Support or /contact for human help.

## Jobs

**Clients**
- Create jobs as draft or publish them open.
- Set scope, category, and budget (fixed, range, or hourly).
- Before publish, the Post Job page shows a preflight checklist.
- Open jobs run trust and payment checks: active account, verified email, profile completeness (default at least 60%), phone on profile, ID verification when required, wallet topped up at least once, wallet balance at least the greater of the default minimum (100 Br unless admins change it) and the job budget, and limits on open jobs (default 10) and new open jobs per day (default 5).
- Review proposals or send hire invitations; accepted work becomes a contract.
- Manage jobs, proposals, invitations, and contracts from the client dashboard.
- Client coin rewards for job posting are tied to completed contract work, not only publishing a listing.

**Freelancers**
- Find Jobs lists open public jobs from clients who pass trust checks.
- Job cards can show client trust signals (for example email, ID, payment verified, member since).
- Apply with a proposal (bid and cover letter) or respond to hire invitations.
- After hire, deliver through the contract flow (submissions and client review).
- Freelancers earn coins when awarded a job (proposal accepted or hire invitation accepted).

**Safety**
- Freelancers can report a job for admin review.
- Admins can close jobs and suspend accounts from admin tools.

## Services, products, wallet

**Services**
- Freelancers publish services with pricing and delivery expectations.
- Clients discover services on the public Services pages and place service orders in the app.
- Service work is coordinated through orders, messaging, and disputes when needed.

**Digital products**
- Sellers publish digital products such as templates, courses, graphics, and similar assets.
- Buyers purchase in-app; clients use cart and buying flows where available.
- After purchase, buyers access deliverables in Dashboard → My courses (hosted file download and/or external course link from the seller).
- Sellers can upload a full course file or provide an external link; promotion text is public while full access unlocks after purchase.
- Seller coin rewards apply on product sale.

**Wallet and payments**
- Each member can hold an on-platform wallet balance in Birr.
- Clients use Payments and wallet areas for top-up, history, and job-related spending.
- Freelancers and sellers receive net payouts after platform commission on completed work and sales.
- Wallet supports top-up, withdraw, transfer, and transaction history (exact external payment rails depend on deployment).
- A client's first successful wallet top-up marks payment as verified for job publishing.

**Commission**
- Platform commission defaults to 10% and is admin-configurable.
- Buyers and clients see the full agreed job or product price; commission is deducted from freelancer or seller earnings at payout, not hidden in the buyer price.

## Coins and commission (defaults)

Coins are separate from the Birr wallet. Typical defaults (admins can change rates and rules):

- Client coins after job-related reward tied to completed contract work (not only posting).
- Freelancer coins when awarded a job.
- Seller coins on product sale.
- Spend coins at checkout for up to 30% of the order total, subject to balance.
- Default exchange: 20 coins = 1 Birr unless admins change the rate.
- Clients track coins in Dashboard → Coins / rewards.
